The KM Consultancy Concept

Let's be clear: you do not need to hire a coach, and you do not need a personal trainer to get into the best shape of your life.
Everything you need to build a healthy, strong and capable body is available right here on this website — completely free.
If you take the core Basics of Training principles outlined here and apply them consistently, you will achieve incredible results all on your own.

So What Is Personal Fitness Consultancy?

I promise I'm not just trying to sound fancy. Personal fitness consultancy is a well-rounded approach to your health and fitness with an emphasis on education and understanding.

Together we tailor nutrition and training programs to you, your body, your schedule and your goals. Everything we do has a purpose and my goal is to make sure you actually understand why you are doing what you are doing.

When you understand the logic behind your routine, your motivation and discipline skyrocket. Over time, this builds total autonomy—giving you the skills to easily manage your own fitness for the rest of your life.

The ultimate goal To teach you enough that you eventually no longer need me.

How Consultancy Differs from Traditional Personal Training

Traditional personal training is built to keep you coming back forever. You pay a premium, show up to a session, blindly do whatever exercises the trainer tells you to do, and leave without ever understanding why you did them. The second you travel for work, get busy, or stop paying for sessions, your progress flatlines.

We do things differently. Our ultimate goal is your complete autonomy — so you can manage your training and nutrition by yourself, or at least fully understand how to, so you can adapt it on the fly when life gets in the way.

Your Roadmap:
How It Works

Your journey towards fitness autonomy begins here.

  1. 01

    The Strategy Consultation

    We begin with a deep-dive into your lifestyle, schedule, and fitness levels. We map how to inject simple, high-yield principles of training and nutrition directly into your existing week
    without it feeling like a second job.

  2. 02

    Optional In-Person Sessions

    Hands-on technique coaching if you want it. Early on we may meet a few times to master lifting form and build confidence. Later, sessions become educational workshops—you learn how to program your own training, not just follow workouts.

  3. 03

    Autonomy & Lifelong Maintenance

    You will fully understand how to modify workouts when you are short on time, traveling, or stuck in a hotel with minimal equipment. Fitness becomes a rewarding, natural part of everyday life—not a chore or exhausting diet.

Choose Your Speed

Complete individual flexibility. There are no locked-in contracts—you choose exactly how much support you want.

The Quick Launch

Some clients only book one or two consultation sessions. They get the clarity they need, map out their plan, and confidently manage their own training from there.

The Accountable Partnership

Other clients prefer a more hands-on approach. They want planning handled for them and use regular check-ins to stay motivated, consistent, and on track.

Whether you need a one-off blueprint or ongoing guidance, the goal remains identical: small, manageable lifestyle tweaks that compound into massive results over time.
